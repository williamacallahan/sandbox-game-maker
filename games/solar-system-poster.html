☀ The Sun

The Sun contains 99.86% of all mass in the solar system. Its core reaches 15 million °C — hot enough to fuse hydrogen into helium.

🪐 Gas Giants

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm larger than two Earths that has raged for over 350 years. Saturn's rings are made mostly of ice and rock.

🌍 Habitable Zone

Earth sits in the "Goldilocks Zone" — just the right distance from the Sun for liquid water to exist, making it the only known planet to harbour life.

🌌 Scale

If the Sun were the size of a basketball, Earth would be a grape seed 26 metres away, and Neptune would be a pea over 750 metres away.
